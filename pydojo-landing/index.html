What's inside

Four ways to train,
one adaptive engine.

The dojo doesn't just quiz you — it watches which concepts you miss, weights them heavier, and reintroduces them on a spaced-repetition schedule until they stick.

Predict the output

Read a snippet, type exactly what it prints. Trains you to simulate the interpreter in your head — the single most-underrated skill for debugging.

Spot the bug

One realistic bug hides in every snippet. Mutating-while-iterating, closure late binding, mutable defaults — the classics that keep re-appearing in real code.

Read the traceback

Given the error and stack, explain the root cause. Because reading a Python traceback well is 80% of the debugging job.

Explain the code

Describe what a snippet does in plain English. Graded by AI on whether you captured the key concept — not on wording.

Curriculum builder New

Three presets, or generate a custom multi-week plan from a goal ("master list mutations in 4 weeks"). Weeks unlock as you hit mastery targets.

Boss speed rounds

Six timed predict-output challenges in 100 seconds. Speed earns bonus XP. Great for warm-ups or ending a session.

Daily challenge

One deterministic puzzle per day, double XP. Same puzzle for everyone that day, then the streak resets.

Lab (BYOC)

Paste your own Python — run it, step through it line by line, get an AI explanation, or turn it into a bug-hunt exercise.

Real execution

Every "Run" and "Step" uses Pyodide — a real CPython in your browser. No mocked outputs, no server, no signup.

How it works

Practice · track · adapt.

The dojo turns the abstract goal of "get better at Python" into a specific, measurable feedback loop.

01

Answer a snippet

Pick a mode (predict, bug, traceback, explain) and difficulty, or let a curriculum pick for you. Each snippet is small enough to reason about, hard enough to teach something.

02

Get graded honestly

Exact string match for output, AI grading for free-text answers, real Python execution for your Lab code. Progressive hints available (with a small XP cost).

03

Watch the engine adapt

Missed the mutable-default gotcha? It resurfaces sooner and heavier. Nailed closures three times? They fade to the back of the queue. The path is yours.

Get sharper this week.

The dojo runs entirely in your browser. Your progress saves locally, no account required, and you can export it as JSON any time.
